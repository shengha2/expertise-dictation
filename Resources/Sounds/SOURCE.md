# Minimal dictation cues

`start.mp3` and `stop.mp3` are the paired Minimal cues from UI SFX by Romain Simon / Yuki Capital.

- Source: https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx
- Pinned revision: `9950fe66f993a6660dab9c2651dcbcd899ffd83b`
- Paths: `packages/uisfx/sounds/minimal/start.mp3` and `packages/uisfx/sounds/minimal/stop.mp3`
- License: CC0 1.0; the original dedication is in `LICENSE-UI-SFX.txt`.
- Start indicates that audio capture is ready. Stop indicates capture has ended; it does not imply successful transcription or insertion.

The older WAV cues are retained for compatibility; the player prefers the approved Minimal MP3 pair. Cancellation is silent.
